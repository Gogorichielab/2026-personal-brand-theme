One place for decisions can save a surprising amount of repeated work.

A project notebook does not need a special app. A plain text file works if it is easy to find and pleasant enough to keep using.

Mine has three sections: what I am trying, what happened, and what I will try next. That is enough structure to make yesterday's thinking available tomorrow.

## Record the reason

Writing “changed the navigation” tells a future reader very little. Writing “moved the archive link because readers could not find older posts” preserves the reason behind the change.

A useful notebook is a record of decisions, including the ones that did not work. It is less polished than documentation and often more revealing.
