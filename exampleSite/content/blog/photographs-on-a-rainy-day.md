Grey weather offers its own collection of colors, shapes, and reflections.

Rain cancelled my plan for a bright afternoon photo walk. I went out anyway, with a shorter route and a waterproof pocket for the camera.

The best subjects were close to the ground: reflected windows, red brake lights, and leaves pressed flat against the pavement.

## Choose a constraint

I decided to photograph only reflections. Having one thing to look for made an ordinary street feel full of possibilities.

A constraint can be a useful starting point for creative work. It narrows the first decision enough that you can begin, while leaving plenty to discover along the way.
