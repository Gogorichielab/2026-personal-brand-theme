Old pages deserve a little attention alongside new posts.

I spent an afternoon browsing my own site as if I had never seen it before. Some links were vague, one project description was out of date, and a good older post had become difficult to find.

## Tend what is already there

I made a small list:

1. Replace unclear link text.
2. Add a related article to an older post.
3. Mark a finished experiment as archived.
4. Remove a recommendation I no longer use.

None of these changes needed an announcement. Together, they made the site more useful.

Publishing is the beginning of a page's life. Occasional care helps it remain worth visiting.
