Example content helps people understand both the possibilities and the limits of a tool.

A demo is often the first documentation someone reads. They click through it, inspect a page, and imagine where their own work would fit.

That makes ordinary examples valuable. A short post, a longer article, a list of projects, and a page with a few headings answer practical questions faster than a feature list.

## Show realistic variety

Example content should exercise the paths people will actually use. Different title lengths reveal layout decisions. Multiple pages reveal pagination. Tags show how a collection can be explored.

A good demo gives visitors enough information to decide what to try next, and enough context to begin.
