A modest weekly routine can carry an idea further than a grand launch plan.

My first plan for a side project assumed I would suddenly have several free evenings every week. The second plan fit into one quiet hour on Sunday.

That hour had a small purpose: leave the project a little easier to continue.

## End with a next step

Before closing the laptop, I wrote one concrete action for the next session. “Improve the site” became “write the empty state for the reading list.”

Some weeks the only progress was clarifying a question. That still reduced the effort needed to start again.

A sustainable project has room for the rest of life. The pace can be slow and still be a pace.
