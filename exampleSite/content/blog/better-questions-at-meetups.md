A specific opening question can make a room full of strangers easier to navigate.

“What do you do?” is a perfectly reasonable question, but it can turn an introduction into a job description.

At a recent meetup, I tried asking what people had enjoyed learning lately. The answers included sourdough, a database migration, and repairing an old bicycle.

## Make an easy opening

A good question leaves room for a short answer. People should be able to join a conversation without presenting their whole biography.

A few prompts I want to keep:

- What brought you along tonight?
- Have you made anything just for fun recently?
- What would you like to learn next?

The goal is a conversation that both people can shape.
