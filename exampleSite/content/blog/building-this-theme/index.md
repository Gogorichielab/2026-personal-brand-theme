Most blog themes start with a list of posts. I wanted one that starts with a person: who they are, what they care about, and where to find their work.

![Abstract cover illustration in the theme's blue palette](cover.png)

The image above lives next to this post in a page bundle. The theme's image render hook converts it to WebP and adds width and height so the page doesn't jump while it loads.
