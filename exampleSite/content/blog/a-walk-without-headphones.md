A familiar route feels different when there is nothing playing in your ears.

I left my headphones at home and took the long route to the shops. Without a podcast setting the pace, I noticed a hand-painted sign, a new patch of wildflowers, and a bench I had walked past for years.

## Leave a little room

Not every spare minute needs an input. Sometimes an empty stretch of time gives an unfinished idea enough space to become a useful question.

The experiment was small: twenty minutes, no destination worth optimizing, and no need to record anything. I came home with bread and the outline of a project I had been avoiding.
